Jimi Cohen

World record tree planter. Founder of Treegens. Host of Eco Champions. Keynote speaker.

Short bio

Jimi Cohen is an Australian world-record tree planter, founder of Treegens, and host of the Eco Champions podcast. On Earth Day, 22 April 2024, in Kwale County, Kenya, he planted 30,469 mangroves in 24 hours, above the previous record of 23,060.

Long bio

Jimi grew up in Avalon, Australia, where his mother's work protecting local koalas taught him early that one determined person can bend the future of an ecosystem.

That lesson became a life. Through We Make Impact, he worked on water security and community development projects. Then came the trees: founding Treegens, pioneering verified, rewarded planting across East Africa and beyond, and putting his own body on the line for a 24 hour world record on the Kenyan coast.

Today he splits his time between the mud of mangrove sites, the mic of the Eco Champions podcast, and stages around the world, all in service of one idea: regeneration should be the most rewarding work on Earth.

On Earth Day 2024, on the coast of Kwale County in Kenya, Jimi planted from one sunrise to the next and set the official world record for the most mangrove trees planted by an individual in 24 hours: 30,469. It took three separate attempts. The result is documented in independent coverage. Antoine Moses reclaimed the title with 47,460 mangroves on 30 April 2026, as shown in the official current record listing. Jimi reports planting faster than that pace in test runs; this is a first-party training claim, not a certified result. The next official attempt is not a published schedule.

Campaign structure

The proposal is built around a campaign rather than a single event. Jimi intends to keep pursuing the record if an attempt falls short, and each filmed attempt can create sponsor content of its own.

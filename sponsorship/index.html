The story

A record made to be broken back.

On Earth Day 2024, on the coast of Kwale County in Kenya, Jimi planted from one sunrise to the next and set the official world record for the most mangrove trees planted by an individual in 24 hours: 30,469, beating the previous mark by more than 7,000 trees. It took him three separate attempts to get there. The result is documented in independent coverage.

April 2024 · Kwale, Kenya Jimi breaks the world record

30,469 mangroves in 24 hours, planted by one person. Certified on Earth Day, 22 April 2024.

April 2026 · Mombasa, Kenya The record is raised to 47,460

The planter whose record Jimi broke came back and reclaimed the title with 47,460 mangroves in a day, as shown in the official current record listing.

Training runs · 2026 Jimi reports beating the new pace in training

Jimi reports planting faster than the pace behind the 47,460 mark in test runs. This is a first-party training claim, not a certified result. The next official attempt is not a published schedule.
